* Arrivals in the roster log now carry their real date. Every change used to
  be stamped with the moment the app happened to look: after three days away,
  all three arrivals bore the timestamp of the reopening.
* The API had always supplied that date — guild.php's <joined> — but both apps
  threw it away, not knowing its unit. It is a counter of tenths of a second.
* That counter drifts, so the decoded date does not decide alone: it is fitted
  between the reading that did not yet see the newcomer and the one that did.
  The API says nothing about departures or ranks: those lines keep the date
  they were noticed.
