* The bell now stays in place, for characters and guilds alike. It only showed
  up when an alert fired: with no threshold set there was no alert, so nothing
  on screen ever revealed that thresholds existed.
* Silent, it still opens and says what it watches — a long press on a material
  or a piece of equipment sets a threshold, and nearly full containers report
  themselves. Ringing, it carries its count in a badge.
* The home screen shows each entity's count on its card, read from the cache
  with no network: you can see which one needs attention without opening it.
