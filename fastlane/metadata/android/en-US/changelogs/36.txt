* Guild money now enters and leaves the log like items do. Coin has neither a
  sheet nor a quality, but it moves, and that is all the log asks for.
* Movements carry the date of the reading, not the date of the sync. The API
  does not recompute a feed on demand: it serves the last one it cached,
  sometimes ten hours old. Checking in every evening around the same time gave
  a log where every day bore the same hour.
* The log reads in columns: icon, quality and amount line up instead of
  trailing the item name. Chest labels are cut at the bracket the API almost
  never closes.
